Medical training for the first critical minutes

During a medical incident, recognition, communication and initial action shape what remains possible. Security Control delivers practical medical training for those responsible until professional care becomes available.

01/Approach

Training for the context

An office in the Netherlands, international travel and a remote site require different preparation. We align scenarios, equipment and decisions with response time, participant role and available support.

02/Scope

Potential subjects

  • 01Initial assessment and alerting
  • 02CPR and use of an AED
  • 03Control of severe bleeding
  • 04Airway problems and common acute conditions
  • 05Handover to professional medical services
  • 06Teamwork, communication and medical equipment
  • 07Workplace first aid (EHBO VCA)
  • 08Use of fire extinguishers and fire blankets

03/Experience

Experience where help is not always close

International operational experience sharpens awareness of time, logistics and limited resources. We bring that reality into exercises without unnecessary drama or false confidence.

04/Standard

Quality and boundaries

Level, instructor qualifications, objectives, equipment and any certification are confirmed in advance. Training does not replace professional medical care; it prepares participants to act responsibly until that care is available.
