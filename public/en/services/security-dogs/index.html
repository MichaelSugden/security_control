Security dogs for visible deterrence and focused control

A professional dog team extends observation, deterrence and coverage where conventional guarding alone may be insufficient. Security Control deploys security dogs only within a defined task, with a suitable handler, clear procedures and careful regard for the environment.

01/Scope

Where dog teams add value

  • 01Large sites and perimeter security
  • 02Construction, storage and vacant properties
  • 03Events and temporary sites
  • 04Preventative patrols during elevated risk
  • 05Support to existing security teams

02/Approach

Dog and handler as one team

Quality depends on the partnership. We assess task, site, public interaction, working hours and sensitivities before selecting a team. Qualifications and conditions are confirmed in advance.

03/Standard

Safe and proportionate

Routes, rest periods, handovers, public contact and escalation are documented. Deployment must make a demonstrable contribution and comply with law and site rules.

04/Experience

Experience across operating environments

International operational experience supports effective integration at sites with multiple suppliers, languages and security protocols, without ambiguity over command.
