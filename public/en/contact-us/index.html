  1. Contact

Discuss your security requirement in complete confidence

An initial conversation does not need to contain every detail. Share only what is needed to understand the reason, urgency and desired support. We can then agree an appropriate channel for further sensitive information.

01/Scope

Reasons to contact us

  • 01Executive protection and close protection
  • 02Family Security Programmes
  • 03Property and site security
  • 04Event security and specialist capability
  • 05Security equipment and training
  • 06International travel or security operations

02/Approach

What to expect

You will receive a personal response. During the assessment, we discuss context, timing, countries or locations, stakeholders and confidentiality. Once the requirement is clear, we recommend next steps or an appropriate assessment.

03/Discretion

Handling information safely

Do not include names of protected persons, exact travel information, security procedures or other operationally sensitive information in a general form. These details should be discussed through an appropriate channel.

04/Contact

Your message will be handled personally and discreetly.

Please do not include names of protected persons, exact travel information or security procedures in this form.

We use your details only to handle your message, as described in our Privacy Policy.
